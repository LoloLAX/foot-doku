Ahmed Nadhir Benbouali (Arabic: أحمد نذير بن بوعلي; born 17 April 2000) is an Algerian professional footballer who plays as a forward for Nemzeti Bajnokság I club Győr and the Algeria national team.

Club career

[edit]

On 23 July 2022, Benbouali joined Belgian club Charleroi, signing a three-year contract with the club until 2025.[1]

On 3 September 2024, Benbouali was loaned by Hungarian club Győr.[2] On 22 September 2024, he scored twice in a 2–1 victory over Paksi FC.[3] On 16 May 2026, he netted the only goal in a 1–0 away win for Győr over Kisvárda, securing his club's 5th league title on the final matchday.[4]
