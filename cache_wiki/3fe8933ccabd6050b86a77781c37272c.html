Jaminton Leandro Campaz (born 24 May 2000) is a Colombian professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder for Argentine Primera División club Rosario Central and the Colombia national team.[2]

Club career

[edit]

Campaz joined Campeonato Brasileiro Série A club Grêmio from Deportes Tolima in August 2021, having signed a contract until December 2025.[3]

International career

[edit]

Campaz was selected for the Colombia national team for 2021 Copa América and made his debut on 17 June 2021 in a match against Venezuela.[4]
