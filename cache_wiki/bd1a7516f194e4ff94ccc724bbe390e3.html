Noor Al-Deen Mahmoud Ali Al-Rawabdeh (Arabic: نُور الدِّيْن مَحمُود عَلِيّ الرَّوَّابدَة; born 24 February 1997) is a Jordanian professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Malaysia Super League club Selangor and the Jordan national team.

International career

[edit]

Al-Rawabdeh debuted for the Jordan national team in a 1–1 2019 WAFF Championship tie with Kuwait on 8 August 2019.[2]
