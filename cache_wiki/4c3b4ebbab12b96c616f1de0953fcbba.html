Olwethu Mpilwenhle Makhanya (born 30 April 2004) is a South African professional soccer player who plays as a defender for the Philadelphia Union of Major League Soccer and the South Africa national team.[2]

Career

[edit]

Makhanya started his career with South African side Stellenbosch, where he was regarded to have performed well for the club.[3] He was nominated for South Africa Young Footballer of the Year.[4] He won the 2022-23 Nedbank Cup Most Promising Player award.[5] In 2023, he signed for American side Philadelphia Union, becoming the club's first under-22 initiative signing.[6]

Style of play

[edit]

Makhanya is known for his bravery.[7] He can operate as a central defender in a back four or a back three.[8]

Personal life

[edit]

Makhanya has regarded England international John Stones as one of his soccer idols.[9]
