Ime Daniel Okon (born 20 February 2004) is a South African professional soccer player who plays as a defender for Hannover 96 and the South Africa national team.

Early life

[edit]

Okon was born in 2004 in South Africa. He attended Florida Park High School in South Africa.[2]

Club career

[edit]

Okon started his career with South African side SuperSport United.[3] He joined German club Hannover 96 ahead of the 2025–26 season on a four-year contract.[4]

International career

[edit]

Okon made his debut for the South Africa national team on 4 June 2025 in the opening match of the 2025 COSAFA Cup against Mozambique.[5] He scored his first goal three days later in the second tournament match against Zimbabwe.[6]

Personal life

[edit]

Okon is a native of Florida, South Africa. He was born to a Nigerian father and South African mother.[7] He is the brother of South African sprinter Udeme Okon.[8]
