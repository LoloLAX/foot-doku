Kerim Alajbegović (pronounced [kěrim alajbêːgoʋitɕ]; born 21 September 2007) is a professional footballer who plays as a winger for Austrian Bundesliga club Red Bull Salzburg. He will join Bundesliga club Bayer Leverkusen on 1 July 2026. Born in Germany, he plays for the Bosnia and Herzegovina national team.

Alajbegović started his professional career at Red Bull Salzburg.

A former youth international for Bosnia and Herzegovina, Alajbegović made his senior international debut in 2025.

Club career

[edit]

Bayer Leverkusen

[edit]

Alajbegović started playing football at his hometown club 1. FC Köln, before joining Bayer Leverkusen's youth academy in 2021. In January 2025, he signed his first professional contract with the team.[2]

Red Bull Salzburg

[edit]

In July, Alajbegović was transferred to Austrian side Red Bull Salzburg for an undisclosed fee.[3] He made his professional debut in a UEFA Champions League qualifier against Brann on 23 July at the age of 17.[4] On 26 July, he scored his first professional goal in an Austrian Cup game against Union Dietach.[5] He made his league debut Ried on 2 August.[6] On 9 August, he scored his first league goal in a triumph over Grazer AK.[7]

In September, he extended his deal with the squad until June 2029.[8]

Return to Bayer Leverkusen

[edit]

In March 2026, Bayer Leverkusen announced that Alajbegović would return to the club in the following season on a five-year contract.[9]

International career

[edit]

Alajbegović represented Bosnia and Herzegovina at all youth levels. He also served as a captain of the under-17 team under coach Nedim Jusufbegović.[10]

In August 2025, he received his first senior call up, for 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifiers against San Marino and Austria.[11] He debuted against the former on 6 September and managed to score a goal.[12]

In June 2026, Alajbegović was named in Bosnia and Herzegovina's squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[13]

Personal life

[edit]

Alajbegović's parents were born in Bugojno.[14] He has a younger brother, Emre.[15]
