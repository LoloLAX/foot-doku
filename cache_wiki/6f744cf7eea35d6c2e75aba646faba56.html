Diego Alexander Gómez Amarilla (born 27 March 2003) is a Paraguayan professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Premier League club Brighton & Hove Albion and the Paraguay national team.[2] A versatile player, he can be deployed as a defensive midfielder, central midfielder, attacking midfielder or winger.

Club career

[edit]

Libertad

[edit]

Gómez made his senior debut for Libertad on 4 May 2022 against Caracas FC in the Copa Libertadores. Shortly after he made his league debut for the club against Club Olimpia, where he was sent-off. Despite this, he became a regular in the centre of midfield for Libertad by June 2022.[3] In August 2022 he scored his first senior league goals, scoring twice against Club 12 de Octubre SD.[4] Gómez was awarded the best under-19 player award by Paraguayan news organisation Versus in 2022.[5]

Inter Miami

[edit]

On 20 July 2023, Gómez signed with Major League Soccer side Inter Miami for an undisclosed fee.[6] On 21 February 2024, Gómez scored his first goal for the club in a 2–0 win over Real Salt Lake in the first game of the 2024 Major League Soccer season with the help of an assist from Luis Suárez.[7]

Brighton and Hove Albion

[edit]

On 10 December 2024, Gómez agreed to join Premier League club Brighton & Hove Albion on 1 January 2025 until 30 June 2030, in a deal believed to be worth about £14 million. He made his official debut on 19 January 2025 in a 3–1 win against Manchester United.[8] On 25 May 2025, he netted his first Premier League goal in a 4–1 away win over Tottenham Hotspur on the final matchday of the 2024–25 season.[9]

On 23 September 2025, Gómez scored four goals in Brighton's 6–0 away win against Barnsley in the third round of the EFL Cup.[10]

International career

[edit]

On 31 August 2022, at the age of 19, Gómez made his debut for the Paraguay senior team against Mexico. Later that year, he played as the Paraguay under-20 team secured victory at the 2022 South American Games held in Asunción,[11] he was also part of the U20 team at the 2023 South American U-20 Championship in Colombia.

In February 2024, he captained Paraguay U-23 team, and finished as the competition's joint top goalscorer with five goals, at the 2024 CONMEBOL Pre-Olympic Tournament as his side won the tournament and a place at the 2024 Olympic Games.[12] On 10 September, he scored his first senior international goal in a 1–0 victory over Brazil in Paraguay's CONMEBOL 2026 FIFA World Cup qualification campaign.[13]
