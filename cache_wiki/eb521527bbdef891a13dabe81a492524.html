Dominique Célidor Simon (born 29 July 2000) is a professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder for Slovak First Football League club Tatran Prešov. Born in France, he plays for the Haiti national team.

Club career

[edit]

Simon is a product of the youth academies of the French clubs Paris Saint-Germain and Aubervilliers. He made one appearance with Aubervilliers in 2019, before moving to the Spanish club Navarro in 2021.[2] On 23 February 2022, he joined the Estonian Meistriliiga club Paide on a professional contract, and helped them win the 2021–22 Estonian Cup.[3] On 31 January 2024, he moved to the Erovnuli Liga club Dinamo Tbilisi.[4]

On 18 August 2024, he joined the Czech First League club Pardubice.[5] On 22 August 2025, he joined Tatran Prešov on a year-long loan in the Slovak First Football League.

International career

[edit]

Born in France, Simon is of Haitian descent.[1] He was called up to the Haiti national team for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[6]
