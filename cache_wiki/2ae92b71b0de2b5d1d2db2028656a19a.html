Themba Zwane, also known as Mshishi (born 3 August 1989) is a South African professional soccer player who plays as an attacking midfielder for Premier Soccer League club Mamelodi Sundowns and the South Africa national team.

On the 11th of October 2024 he earned his 50th international cap for South Africa.[2]
