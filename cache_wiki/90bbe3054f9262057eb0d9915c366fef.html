Odeh Burhan Shehadeh Fakhoury (Arabic: عودة برهان شحادة الفاخوري; born 22 November 2005) is a Jordanian footballer who plays as a forward for Egyptian Premier League club Pyramids and the Jordan national team.

Club career

[edit]

Early career

[edit]

Born in Amman, Al-Fakhouri began his football career through schools and youth centers funded by Prince Ali bin Al Hussein, where he was one of 8 other 6-year olds chosen to train at UE Cornellà for a short period, with the late Mahmoud El-Gohary recognizing his abilities around that time.[2] After returning to Jordan, he joined Jordan Knights for 8-years. He also briefly joined Al-Faisaly. However, he described that period as unjustly, but also received awards for best player in two tournaments in Palestine and Qatar during that brief period.[2]

Since then, he became a youth product of Amman FC, where he represented the club at the under-15, under-17, and under-19 levels.[2]

Amman FC

[edit]

Al-Fakhouri began playing with the first team of Jordanian First Division League side Amman FC team since 2021 as a 15-year old, as he was recognized for his unique talents within the club.[2]

In a closely-fought 2–1 loss to Jordanian Pro League side Al-Hussein during the 2023–24 Jordan FA Cup, Al-Fakhouri found himself beating a cup record, as he became the youngest goal scorer in the cup's history at 17-years old.[3]

He had received an offer from a Cypriot club during his time at Amman FC. However, he rejected the offer.[3]

Al-Hussein

[edit]

On 15 August 2024, Al-Fakhouri agreed to permanently join Jordanian Pro League side Al-Hussein on a 3-year contract.[4] He started with the club's B team, which participated in the 2024 Jordanian First Division League. There, he was able to score three goals in the competition.

The following season, Al-Fakhouri participated in both legs of the 2025 Jordan Super Cup, which his club defeated Al-Wehdat as a result.[5] On 23 August 2025, Al-Fakhouri put out a notable performance, when he scored and assisted a goal during the club's 4–1 win against Shabab Al-Ordon.[6] By the time the 2025–26 Jordanian Pro League went on break, Odeh had registered 4 goals in 9 appearances.[7] On 23 December 2025, Al-Fakhorui scored two goals during the 2025–26 AFC Champions League Two campaign in Al-Hussein's 3–1 win against Ahal.[8][9][10]

After the success his national team achieved at the 2025 FIFA Arab Cup, Al-Fakhouri gained significant interest from clubs around Egypt, as well as those around Europe.[11] Al Ahly initially showed strong interest in the player, but despite Al-Hussein accepting the fee, Al-Fakhouri feared that he wouldn't get many opportunities at the club and stalled negotiations as a result.[11] Pyramids then emerged as a club interested in Al-Fakhouri's services, with an increased chance of playing at a higher level, as well as a stronger financial promise.[11]

Odeh bid farewell to Al-Hussein during the 4–0 win against Al-Jazeera, with the posibility of moving to either Al-Ahly, Pyramids, or to Europe, with the rumoured club being from the Croatian Football League.[12]

Pyramids

[edit]

On 3 February 2026, Al-Fakhouri joined Egyptian Premier League club Pyramids for an undisclosed fee.[13][14] On 8 February 2026, Al-Fakhouri made his debut for the club during a CAF Champions League matchup against Rivers United, scoring a goal as a substitute and helping the club secure a 4–1 victory.[15]

International career

[edit]

Youth career

[edit]

Al-Fakhouri is a youth international for Jordan, beginning his international career with the Jordan national under-20 football team.[16] During the 2025 AFC U-20 Asian Cup qualification process, he found himself among the goalscorers of a 7–0 win against Hong Kong.[17][18] On that period, Al-Fakhouri gained recognition as a star within the U20 national team, after showing his consistent goal-scoring prowess.[19]

On 28 August 2025, Al-Fakhouri was called up to the Jordan national under-23 football team for the 2026 AFC U-23 Asian Cup qualification matches against Bhutan, Chinese Taipei, and Turkmenistan on 3, 6, and 9 September 2025; respectively.[20] He registered a total of 4 goals in 3 games.

On 23 December 2025, Al-Fakhoruri was called up to the 2026 AFC U-23 Asian Cup,[21] participating in all four matches.

Senior career

[edit]

On 2 October 2025, Al-Fakhouri received his first call-up to the Jordan national football team for the friendly matches against Bolivia and Albania on 10 and 14 October 2025; respectively.[22] On 22 November 2025, Al-Fakhouri was called up to the senior team once again to participate in the 2025 FIFA Arab Cup.[23][24] He was regarded as Jamal Sellami's "trump card" leading up to the tournament.[25] He participated primarily as a striker during the tournament, where he had a disallowed goal against Egypt. His role grew throughout the tournament after Yazan Al-Naimat suffered an ACL injury against Iraq.[26]

Personal life

[edit]

Despite both sharing a surname, Odeh is not related to Abdallah Al-Fakhouri.[27] Odeh is also a Jordanian Christian.[28]

Playing style

[edit]

Al-Fakhouri's former manager João Mota described him as a player who is strong in dribbling one-on-ones against defenders, is a quick player, and is bold in the way he plays.[29][30]
