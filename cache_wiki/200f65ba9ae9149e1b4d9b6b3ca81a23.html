Odiljon Hamrobekov (born 13 February 1996) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Tractor and the Uzbekistan national team.[1]

Career

[edit]

Club

[edit]

On 23 July 2021, Hamrobekov returned to Pakhtakor Tashkent, signing a contract until the end of 2022.Currently playing for Bunyodkor FC, another team from the capital city.[2]

International

[edit]

Hamrobekov made his international debut for Uzbekistan on 14 November 2017, during a friendly match against United Arab Emirates.[3]
