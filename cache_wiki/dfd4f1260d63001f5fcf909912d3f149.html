Saleh Hardani (Persian: صالح حردانی; born 26 December 1998), also known as Saadat Hardani, is an Iranian footballer who plays as a right-back for the Persian Gulf Pro League club Esteghlal and the Iran national team.[2] He is younger brother of Saber Hardani.[3]

Club career

[edit]

Foolad

[edit]

He made his debut for Foolad in first fixtures of 2019–20 Iran Pro League against Sanat Naft Abadan.[4]

Esteghlal

[edit]

He joined Esteghlal for 2019–20 Iran Pro League and scored an important goal against his previous club, Foolad, which made Esteghlal the champion of the season.

Personal life

[edit]

On 4 February 2026, Hardani responded to the 2025–2026 Iranian protests and the government's crackdown of the protests on his Instagram, saying: "He who is not sad in this time is either not a human being, or not in this world."[5]
