Salih Özcan (born 11 January 1998) is a professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder. Born in Germany, he plays for the Turkey national team.[2]

Club career

[edit]

Köln

[edit]

Born in Germany, Özcan is of Turkish descent as he was born into a Turkish family. Özcan spent the majority of his youth career in 1. FC Köln's youth system. He first featured in 1. FC Köln's first team in September 2016 in a Bundesliga match against Schalke 04.[3] During the 2017–18 season he consistently started in central midfield. He scored his first league goal on 2 September 2018 in a 2. Bundesliga match against FC St. Pauli.[4]

In 2017, Özcan was awarded the golden Fritz Walter Medal for the best U19 player in Germany.[5]

Loan to Holstein Kiel

[edit]

On 23 August 2019, Özcan joined Holstein Kiel on loan until the end of 2019–20 season.[6] Three days later, he made his debut with the team as a starter in a 2. Bundesliga away match against FC St. Pauli, which ended in a 2–1 defeat.[7]

Return to Köln

[edit]

On 29 June 2021, in spite of speculations about a possible transfer, the club announced that Özcan had signed a new two-year contract with Köln.[8]

Borussia Dortmund

[edit]

On 23 May 2022, it was reported that Özcan would join Borussia Dortmund for the 2022–23 season and that he would sign a contract with the club until 2026. The German sports magazine kicker estimated the transfer fee to be €5 million.[9]

On 27 August 2022, he made his debut with the team as a starter in a Bundesliga match against Hertha BSC.[10] On 6 September 2022, he made his continental debut with the team in a UEFA Champions League match against København.[11]

Loan to Wolfsburg

[edit]

On 28 August 2024, Özcan moved on loan to VfL Wolfsburg.[12] Three days later, he made his debut with Wolfsburg in a 2–0 away win against his former club Holstein Kiel.[13]

Return to Borussia Dortmund

[edit]

In late January 2025, Özcan was recalled to Borussia Dortmund following the long-term injury of Felix Nmecha.[14] In March 2026, the club announced that his contract, which was due to expire at the end of the season, would not be renewed.[15] He left the club at the end of the 2025–26 season, with his final game being on May 8 in a 3–2 win over Eintracht Frankfurt where he came off the bench in the 78th minute for Marcel Sabitzer.[16]

International career

[edit]

Özcan was eligible for both the Turkish and German national teams,[17] and played for Germany's youth teams.[18] He played his last game for Germany U21 on 6 June 2021, starting in the UEFA European Under-21 Championship final.[19]

On 25 March 2022, Özcan was called up to the Turkey national team by head coach Stefan Kuntz.[20] He debuted in a friendly 3–2 loss to Italy on 29 March 2022.[21]

On 7 June 2024, he was named in the final squad of 26 players selected by Vincenzo Montella to take part in UEFA Euro 2024.[22] On 18 November 2025, he scored his first national goal against Spain in the 2026 FIFA World Cup qualification.[23]
