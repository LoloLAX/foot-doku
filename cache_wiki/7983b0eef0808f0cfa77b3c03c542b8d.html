Jeremy Cornelis Jacobus Antonisse (born 29 March 2002) is a professional footballer who plays as a left winger for Greek Super League club A.E. Kifisia. Born in the Netherlands, he plays for the Curaçao national team.[1]

Club career

[edit]

A youth academy graduate of PSV, Antonisse signed his first professional contract with the club in August 2019.[2] He signed a three-year contract through to the summer of 2022.[3]

On 30 November 2020, Antonisse made his professional debut for Jong PSV in a 1–0 league defeat to SC Cambuur.[4] He made his senior debut for PSV on 26 January 2021 in a 2–0 league win against FC Emmen.[5] On 23 August 2021, he signed a contract extension with club until June 2024.[6] On 31 January 2023, he joined FC Emmen on a loan deal until the end of the season.[7]

On 1 September 2023, Primeira Liga club Moreirense announced the signing of Antonisse on a four-year contract.[8] In July 2025, he joined newly promoted Greek Super League club A.E. Kifisia.[9]

International career

[edit]

Born in the Netherlands, Antonisse is of Curaçaoan descent.[10] He has previously represented Netherlands at youth level.[11] On 16 October 2019, he was named in stand-by list for the 2019 FIFA U-17 World Cup.[12]

In March 2021, Antonisse received his first call-up to the Curaçao national team for the 2022 FIFA World Cup qualifying matches.[13] He made his debut on 25 March in a 5–0 win over Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.[14] In June 2021, he was named in Curaçao's 42-man preliminary squad for the 2021 CONCACAF Gold Cup.[15]

In June 2025, Antonisse was named in the squad for the 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup.[16] In May 2026, he was named in Curaçao's squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, the country's first-ever appearance at the tournament.[17]
