Abdulla Abdullaev (Uzbek: Abdulla Qahramonjon oʻgʻli Abdullayev, Абдулла Қаҳрамонжон ўғли Абдуллаев; born 1 September 1997) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays for Dibba and the Uzbekistan national team.

Career

[edit]

International

[edit]

Abdullaev made his debut for the Uzbekistan main team on 5 September 2021 in a Friendly match against Sweden.[2] Named to the Uzbekistan national team squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[3]
