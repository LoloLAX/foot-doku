Ali Hassan Majrashi (Arabic: علي حسن مجرشي; born 1 October 1999) is a Saudi Arabian footballer who plays as a right back for Saudi Pro League side Al-Ahli and the Saudi Arabia national team.[2][3]

Career

[edit]

Majrashi started his career at Al-Shabab and is a product of Al-Shabab's youth system. On 30 January 2018, Majrashi made his professional debut for Al-Shabab against Al-Ahli in the Pro League, replacing Abdulwahab Jaafer.[4] On 11 July 2019, Majrashi signed a five-year professional contract with Al-Shabab.[5] On 30 June 2019, he was chosen in the Saudi scholarship program to develop football talents established by the General Sports Authority.[6] On 7 February 2021, Majrashi joined Al-Faisaly on loan.[7] He started the 2021 King Cup Final on 27 May 2021 helping Al-Faisaly win their first title.[8] On 25 January 2022, Majrashi joined Al-Ahli on a four-year deal.[9]
