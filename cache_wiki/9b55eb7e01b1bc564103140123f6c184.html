Kim Tae-hyeon (Korean: 김태현; born 17 September 2000) is a South Korean footballer who plays as a left-footed centre-back for J1 League club Kashima Antlers[1] and the South Korea national team.

Career

[edit]

On 28 December 2021, Kim joined Vegalta Sendai on a one year loan.[2]

On 10 January 2024, Kim joined Sagan Tosu.[3]

In January 2025, it was announced that Kim would be joining J1 League club Kashima Antlers following a single season at Sagan Tosu.[4]
