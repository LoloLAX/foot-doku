Azizbek Amonov (Uzbek Cyrillic: Азизбек Аскарович Аманов; born 30 October 1997) is an Uzbek footballer who plays as a midfielder for Buxoro and the Uzbekistan national team.[1][2][3][4][5]

Club career

[edit]

Bunyodkor

[edit]

Azizbek signed a contract with Bunyodkor on 10 January 2025.[6]
