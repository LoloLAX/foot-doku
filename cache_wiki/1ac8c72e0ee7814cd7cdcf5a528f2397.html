Jacob Michael Italiano (born 30 July 2001)[2] is an Australian professional soccer player who plays as a right back for Austrian Bundesliga club Grazer AK and the Australia national team.

He has previously played for Australian club Perth Glory and German club Borussia Mönchengladbach II.

Club career

[edit]

Perth Glory

Borussia Mönchengladbach

[edit]

In May 2019, Italiano moved to German club Borussia Mönchengladbach.[3][4] He played for the reserves side and often trained with the senior team.

Grazer AK

[edit]

In June 2024, Italiano joined newly promoted Austrian club Grazer AK on a three-year contract.[5]

International career

[edit]

Born in Australia, Italiano is dual-citizen of Australia and Italy.[6] He was called up to the Australian national team for a set of friendlies in October 2025, making his debut against Canada.[7][8]
