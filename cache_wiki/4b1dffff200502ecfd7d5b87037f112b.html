Abdulaziz Hatem Mohammed Abdullah (Arabic: عَبْد الْعَزِيز حَاتِم مُحَمَّد عَبْد الله; born 1 January 1990) is a Qatari professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Al Rayyan and captains the Qatar national team.

Club career

[edit]

Hatem began his professional career with Al-Arabi SC in 2007 and moved to Al-Gharafa SC in 2015. In July 2019 he joined Al-Rayyan SC.[3]

International career

[edit]

He made his debut for the Qatar national football team during the 9th International Friendship Tournament held in December 2009.

Hatem participated for the Qatar olympic team in the 2012 Summer Olympic qualifiers. He received 2 yellow cards during the group stage, rendering him ineligible to compete for one game. Qatar, however, chose to play him against Oman, resulting in FIFA overturning the 1–1 draw to a 3–0 loss for Qatar. This meant that Qatar, who were undefeated in the group stage, would end up third in the group while Oman and South Korea went through to the next stage.[4]
