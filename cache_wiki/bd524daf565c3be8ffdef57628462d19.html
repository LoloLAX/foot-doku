Nathaniel Christopher Brown (born 16 June 2003) is a German professional footballer who plays as a left-back for Bundesliga club Eintracht Frankfurt and the Germany national team.

Club career

[edit]

Brown is a youth product of TSV Kümmersbruck and Jahn Regensburg, before joining Nürnberg's youth academy in 2016.[3] On 15 June 2022, he signed his first professional contract with Nürnberg.[4] He began his senior career with Nürnberg's reserves in 2022 in the Regionalliga. On 10 March 2023, he debuted with the senior Nürnberg team in a 2–0 2. Bundesliga win over Eintracht Braunschweig as a late substitute.[5] On 27 June 2023, he extended professional contract with Nürnberg.[6]

On 3 January 2024, Brown signed for Bundesliga club Eintracht Frankfurt on a five-and-a-half-year deal, returning to Nürnberg on loan for the remainder of the season.[7] On 2 November 2024, he scored his first Bundesliga goal in a 7–2 victory over Bochum.[8] He was voted his club's Player of the Season for 2025–26, having recorded four goals and four assists in the league, as well as two assists in the UEFA Champions League.[9]

International career

[edit]

Brown was born in Germany to an American father and German mother.[3] He is a youth international for Germany, having played for the Germany U21s.[10] In October 2025, he was named to the German senior squad.[11] On 10 October 2025, he made his debut for the senior team after coming on as a substitute in a 4–0 win over Luxembourg.[12]

In May 2026, Brown was named in the 26-man squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[13]
