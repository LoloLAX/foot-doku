Ahmed Ali Al-Kassar (Arabic:أحمد علي الكسار) is a Saudi Arabian footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Saudi Arabian club Al-Qadsiah and the Saudi Arabia national team.[1]

Club career

[edit]

Al-Raed

[edit]

On 19 June 2009, Al-Raed signed with Ahmed Al-Kassar from Al-Taraji for 1.8 million Saudi Riyals.[2] He left in 2015 to go to Al-Ettifaq.

Al-Ettifaq

[edit]

On 10 September 2015, Al-Ettifaq signed with Ahmed from Al-Raed.[3] He won the Saudi First Division in his first season. On 18 August 2017, Ahmed Al-Kassar made a last-minute assist for Hazaa Al-Hazaa to help Al-Ettifaq draw 2–2 against Al-Nassr.[4] He became only the fifth goalkeeper to make an assist in the Saudi Professional League.[5] After the match, he said that manager Miodrag Ješić did not want him to go forward, but he ignored that and made the assist.[6]

Al-Fayha

[edit]

On 20 June 2023, Al-Kassar joined Al-Fayha on a free transfer.[7]

Al-Qadsiah

[edit]

On 30 January 2024, Al-Kassar joined Al-Qadsiah on a three-and-a-half-year deal.[8]
