Nkosinathi Emmanuel Sibisi (born 22 September 1995) is a South African soccer player who plays as a defender for South African Premier Division side Orlando Pirates, whom he captains, and the South Africa national team.[2][3]

International career

[edit]

He made his debut for South Africa national soccer team on 10 June 2021 in a friendly against Uganda.[4] In 2022, he appeared in three international friendlies.[5] On the 10th of February 2024 he won AFCON 23 bronze medal with his national team South Africa

On 1 December 2025, Sibisi was called up to the South Africa squad for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.[6]
