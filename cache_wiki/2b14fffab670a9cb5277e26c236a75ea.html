Utkir Yusupov (Uzbek: Oʻtkir Yusupov, Russian: Уткир Юсупов; born 4 January 1991) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Uzbekistan Super League club Navbahor. Born in Kazakhstan, he plays for the Uzbekistan national team.

Career

[edit]

Born in Kazakhstan, Yusupov moved to Uzbekistan aged 5, and gained Uzbekistani citizenship in 2017.[3] He was included in Uzbekistan's squad for the 2019 AFC Asian Cup in the United Arab Emirates.[1] He participated in the 2023 AFC Asian Cup in Qatar as well.
