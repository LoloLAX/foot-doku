Sekotori Evidence Makgopa (born 5 June 2000) is a South African professional soccer player who plays as a forward for South African Premiership side Orlando Pirates and the South Africa national team. He represented the South Africa under-23 team at the 2020 Summer Olympics.

Early life

[edit]

Makgopa was born in GaMampa, near Burgersfort in Limpopo. He attended Poo Secondary School.[3]

Club career

[edit]

Makgopa was scouted by Baroka in 2018, and was promoted to their first team from their development squad in January 2020.[3][4] He made his debut for the club on 23 February 2020 in a 2–2 Nedbank Cup draw with Hungry Lions; Makgopa scored their second goal as they advanced to the next round of the competition after winning the penalty shoot-out.[5] He made his league debut on 1 March 2020 in a 2–1 defeat to Bloemfontein Celtic,[6] He scored his first league goals for the club the following week as he scored a brace in a 2–0 victory over Black Leopards.[6][7] He made 11 appearances for Baroka in all competitions during the 2019–20 season, scoring 5 goals.[6]

International career

[edit]

On 8 June 2021, Makgopa made his debut for South Africa as a substitute against Uganda and scored a brace in a 3–2 win.[8] He represented the South Africa under-23 team at the 2020 Summer Olympics, and scored once in 3 appearances.[9][6]

On 1 December 2025, Makgopa was called up to the South Africa squad for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.[10]
