Brian Gutiérrez (born 17 June 2003) is a professional football player who plays as an attacking midfielder for Liga MX club Guadalajara. Born in the United States, he plays for the Mexico national team.

Club career

[edit]

Chicago Fire

[edit]

Born in Berwyn, Illinois, U.S., Gutiérrez began his career with the Chicago Fire youth academy in 2015.[2] On 9 March 2020, Gutiérrez signed a homegrown player deal with the club's first team in Major League Soccer.[2]

Gutiérrez made his professional debut for the Chicago Fire on 20 August 2020, against Columbus Crew, coming on as an 82nd-minute substitute in a 3–0 defeat.[3]

Guadalajara

[edit]

On 12 December 2025, Gutiérrez joined Mexican club Guadalajara for a reported fee of $5 million.[4]

He made his Liga MX debut on 10 January 2026 in a home match against Pachuca as a starter, contributing an assist at the 17th minute for the first goal of a 2–0 home victory.[5] Gutiérrez scored his first goal with Chivas on March 18, the first in a 5–0 home victory.[6]

International career

[edit]

United States

[edit]

Gutiérrez has played for the US youth national teams at multiple levels, including the under-20 team. He was also in consideration for the USMNT's senior camp in December 2021.[7] On 8 October 2023, Gutiérrez was called up to the under-23 team ahead of friendlies against Mexico and Japan.[8]

In January 2025, Gutiérrez played two friendly matches for the senior United States, against Venezuela and Costa Rica.[citation needed]

Switch to Mexico national team

[edit]

On 6 November 2025, Gutierrez’s management announced the player was considering switching to the Mexico national team.[9]

On 15 January 2026, Gutiérrez was called up by Mexico manager Javier Aguirre and appeared in a friendly match against Panama on 22 January, the same day that FIFA officials authorized his allegiance switch to Mexico.[10][11] On 25 February 2026, Gutierréz scored his first goal for Mexico in a friendly match against Iceland in stoppage time as Mexico took the 4–0 win.[12]

On 31 May, he was named in the final roster for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Personal life

[edit]

Born in the United States, Gutiérrez is of Mexican descent through both of his parents, who are originally from San Juan de los Lagos, Jalisco.[13] He obtained his Mexican citizenship in November 2025, making him eligible to play for the Mexico national team.[14][15]
