Josué Casimir (born 24 September 2001) is a professional footballer who plays as a right-back, right wing-back or striker for French Ligue 1 club Auxerre. Born in Guadeloupe, he represents the Haiti national team.

Club career

[edit]

Casimir made his professional debut with Le Havre in a 1–0 Ligue 2 win over USL Dunkerque on 24 October 2020.[1]

On 6 June 2025, Casimir signed a three-season contract with Auxerre.[2]

International career

[edit]

Casimir was born in the French overseas territory Guadeloupe to Haitian parents.[3] He holds French and Haitian descent.[4] He played for the Guadeloupe U20s at the 2020 CONCACAF U-20 Championship qualifying matches, with three goals in three games.[5] He was called up to the Haiti national team for a set of 2026 FIFA World Cup qualification matches in October 2025.[6]

Personal life

[edit]

Casimir is the younger brother of the Guadeloupean international footballer Stevenson Casimir.[7]
