Antoine Alpha Mendy (born 27 May 2004) is a professional footballer who plays as a right-back and centre-back for Ligue 1 club Nice. Born in France, he plays for the Senegal national team.

Club career

[edit]

Mendy is a youth product of Nice since the age of 10, and worked his way up their youth categories. He began his senior career with their reserves in 2021, and signed his first professional contract with the club on 29 September 2022.[2]

He made his professional and Ligue 1 with Nice as a late substitute in a 1–1 win over Auxerre on 16 October 2022.[3]

In October 2025, he was listed as one of the ESPN soccer's next generation of entertainers.[4]

International career

[edit]

Antoine Mendy received his first call-up to the Senegal national team in 22 March 2025 to take part in the 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifiers.[5]

He made his debut as a starter in a goalless draw against Sudan, then came on as a substitute against Togo, earning praise for his defensive solidity.[6]

On May 21, 2026, Mendy was officially selected by Senegal's coach Pape Thiaw from his list of 28 players to participate in the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[7]

Personal life

[edit]

Born in France, Mendy is of Senegalese descent.[8]
