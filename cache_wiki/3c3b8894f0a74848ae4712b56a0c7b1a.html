Mustafa Saadoon Abbood Al-Korji (Arabic: مصطفى سعدون; born 25 May 2001) is an Iraqi professional footballer who plays as a right back for Iraq Stars League club Al-Shorta and the Iraq national team.

Club career

[edit]

Born in Corgi, Saadoon spent his youth career at the RCD Espanyol football academy in Baghdad. In 2022, he joined Al-Kahrabaa and played there during one season before joining Iraqi giant Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya in August 2023.[2]

International career

[edit]

On 13 October 2023, Saadoon made his international debut for Iraq in the team's goalless draw against Qatar, as part of the 2023 Jordan International Tournament.[3]

In April 2024, Saadoon featured in Iraq U23's squad for the 2024 AFC U-23 Asian Cup. In the last group stage match against Saudi Arabia, he won a penalty for his team and later scored himself the winning goal for Iraq as the match ended in a 2–1 win for them, thus qualify Iraq to the quarter-finals as the group winner despite losing their first game.[4][5] Saadoun continoued to feature for the team as they finished third overall in the tournament and qualified to the 2024 Olympic games
