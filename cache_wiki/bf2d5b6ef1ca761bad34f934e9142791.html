Joel Robert Waterman (born January 24, 1996) is a Canadian soccer player who plays as a centre-back, right-back or defensive midfielder for Chicago Fire of Major League Soccer and the Canada national team.

Early life

[edit]

Waterman began playing soccer at age five with Aldergrove Youth SC.[3][4][5] At age 12, he joined Langley United.[6] Afterwards, he played with Surrey United SC.[7]

When he was 13, he made the British Columbia provincial team.[6] In 2013, he represented British Columbia at the 2013 Canada Summer Games[6] and also had a brief trial with the Vancouver Whitecaps Academy, but did not make the squad.[8]

University career

[edit]

In 2014, he began attending Trinity Western University, where he played for the men's soccer team,[9][10] committing to them when he was in Grade 11.[11] Ahead of his fifth season, he was named the team captain,[12] and helped them win a Canada West silver medal and finished fourth at the National Championship.[13][14] He was also named a Canada West First Team All Star that season.[15][16]

Club career

[edit]

Early career

[edit]

In 2016, Waterman played in the US-based Premier Development League side Kitsap Pumas and made eleven league appearances,[17] while also appearing in two US Open Cup matches.[18] In 2017, Waterman played with Vancouver-based TSS FC Rovers in the PDL, where he made eleven appearances.[19] In 2018, Waterman played for Calgary Foothills in the PDL, making fourteen league appearances and another three in the playoffs as Foothills won the PDL Championship.[20][21]

Cavalry FC

[edit]

At the 2018 CPL–U Sports Draft, Waterman was drafted in the second round by Cavalry FC on November 13, 2018.[22] Initially, he had not planned on declaring for the draft, instead looking at potentially going to Europe or the United States, however, two days before the registration deadline, he decided to register.[23] He officially signed with Cavalry on February 6, 2019.[24][25]

Waterman made his professional debut for Cavalry in their inaugural game against York9 FC on May 4.[26] He scored his first goal for Cavalry on October 5 against HFX Wanderers FC.[27] He helped Cavalry win the Spring and Fall championships[28] and helped them reach the CPL finals, where they finished second,[29] where he was unable to play in the second leg after being sent off in the first leg for a hand ball.[30] In December 2019, Cavalry announced Waterman would return to the club for the 2020 season.[31]

CF Montréal

[edit]

On January 14, 2020, Waterman transferred to Major League Soccer club Montreal Impact.[32][33] With this transfer, Waterman made history as the first ever player from the Canadian Premier League to be sold to an MLS club.[34][35] He made his debut for the Impact on February 19 against Saprissa in a CONCACAF Champions League match.[36] He made his MLS debut for the Impact in their season opener against the New England Revolution on February 29.[37] Upon completion of the 2021 MLS season, CF Montréal announced that they would be exercising the option on Waterman's contract for 2022.[38]

In 2022, he became a regular starter for the club.[39] During the 2022 season, he scored his first goal for Montreal against Orlando City on May 7, netting the opener in an eventual 4-1 victory.[40] On August 4, he scored in the fourth minute of stoppage time to secure a 2-1 win over the Columbus Crew.[41] In March 2023 Waterman signed a new contract with Montreal through 2024, with an option for 2025.[42][43] In November 2024, Waterman would sign a contract extension through the 2027 season, with an option for 2028.[44]

Chicago Fire

[edit]

In August 2025, Waterman was traded to Chicago Fire FC in exchange for $500,000 in General Allocation Money and $100,000 in performance-based money.[45]

International career

[edit]

He was called up to the Canadian senior national team for the first time for a national team camp in January 2021.[46] He earned several more call-ups to the squad during 2021 and 2022.[47][48][49] On November 11, 2022, he made his debut for Canada in a friendly against Bahrain.[50] On November 13, Waterman was named to Canada's squad for the 2022 FIFA World Cup.[51]

In June 2024, Waterman was named to Canada's squad for the 2024 Copa América, as an injury replacement for Junior Hoilett.[52] In July 2025, he was called up to Canada's team for the 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup.[53] In May 2026, Waterman was named to Canada's 26-man squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[54]
