Marwan Attia Fahim Ghallab (Arabic: مروان عطية فاهم غلاب رشوان; born 1 August 1998) is an Egyptian footballer who plays as a midfielder for Egyptian Premier League club Al Ahly and the Egypt national team.

Club career

[edit]

He signed with Al Ahly SC in the middle of the 2022–23 season amid interest from arch-rivals Zamalek SC on a four-and-a-half-year contract.[2][3]

International career

[edit]

He was called up for the first time to the Egyptian national team in August 2022,[4] before making his first appearance on 24 March 2023.[5]

On 2 December 2025, Attia was called up to the Egypt squad for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.[6]

Personal life

[edit]

Attia's father died on 13 July 2023, while Attia was in a camp preparing for the Zamalek match.[7] He married his fiancée, Salma Saqr, on 25 July 2024, after postponing it from last year due to the death of his father.[8]
