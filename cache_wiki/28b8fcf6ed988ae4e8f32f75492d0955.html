Igor Vladimirovich Sergeev (Russian: Игорь Владимирович Сергеев; born 30 April 1993) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays as a forward for Persepolis in the Persian Gulf Pro League and the Uzbekistan national team.

Club career

[edit]

Pakhtakor

[edit]

Sergeev made his Uzbek League debut for Pakhtakor Tashkent on 5 March 2011 against Qizilqum Zarafshon at the Pakhtakor Markaziy Stadium coming on as a 90th-minute substitute for Temurkhuja Abdukholikov as Tashkent won the match 2–0.[2]

Beijing Guoan (loan)

[edit]

On 6 July 2016, Sergeev was loaned to Chinese Super League club Beijing Guoan joining his national team teammate Egor Krimets. On 16 October, he received his first goal during 2016 season in the match against Changchun Yatai.

Return to Pakhtakor

[edit]

On 6 July 2018, Sergeev returned to Pakhtakor Tashkent.[3]

Aktobe

[edit]

On 18 February 2021, FC Aktobe announced the signings of Sergeev.[4]

Tobol

[edit]

On 19 July 2021, Sergeev left Aktobe to sign for Tobol.[5] On 29 July 2021, during a 2021–22 UEFA Europa Conference League 2nd qualifying round game, he scored a brace against Hajduk Split, becoming the first Uzbek to score in the UEFA Conference League.[6] Though his countryman Eldor Shomurodov scored three weeks later, Sergeev remains at 28 years the oldest Uzbek goalscorer in the competition.[7] On 8 June 2023, Sergeev left Tobol by mutual consent.[8]

BG Pathum United

[edit]

On 13 June 2023, BG Pathum United announced the signing of Sergeev.[9]

Second Pakhtakor return

[edit]

On 25 December 2024, Pakhtakor announced the return of Sergeev from BG Pathum United to a one-year contract.[10]

Persepolis F.C.

[edit]

4 January 2026: He accepted Persepolis’ offer and came to Iran. Despite an offer from Sepahan, he chose Iran’s most popular team, Persepolis.[11]

International career

[edit]

Sergeev had established himself as a regular at the under-20 level for Uzbekistan. After the 2012 AFC U-19 Championship Sergeev came out as the top scorer of the tournament with 7 goals throughout as he led Uzbekistan's U20s to the semi-finals, where they lost to South Korea U20s 3–1.[12]

He made his debut for main team, Uzbekistan on 10 September 2013 in 2014 FIFA World Cup qualification match against Jordan. He was named in Uzbekistan's squad for 2015 AFC Asian Cup, held in Australia. Sergeev played three matches and scored Uzbekistan's 1–0 winning goal against North Korea in their opening match in Group B. Sergeev performance with BG Pathum United earn him a call-up to the 2023 AFC Asian Cup held in Qatar.
