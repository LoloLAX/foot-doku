Deiver Andrés Machado Mena (born 2 September 1993) is a Colombian professional footballer who plays as a left-back or left wing-back for Ligue 1 club Nantes and the Colombia national team.

Club career

[edit]

In 2020, Deiver Machado joined Ligue 2 club Toulouse as a free agent.

On 2 July 2021, he joined Ligue 1 club Lens for three years.[2] On 31 January, he extended his contract with Lens until 2026.[3]

On 8 December 2025, Machado joined Ligue 1 club Nantes on a transfer, signing a three-year contract.[4]

International career

[edit]

Machado was named in the Colombia national team's provisional squad for Copa América Centenario but was cut from the final squad.[5]
