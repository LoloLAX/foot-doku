Lukáš Horníček (born 13 July 2002) is a Czech professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Primeira Liga club Braga and the Czech Republic national team.

Club career

[edit]

Horníček is a youth product of the academies of the Czech clubs Sokol Dobříkov and Pardubice. On 19 July 2019, he signed on a trial loan with the Portuguese club Braga for one year with an option to buy.[1] On 24 July 2020, he signed permanently with Braga in what was Pardubice's biggest sale in their history.[2] He was named the best goalkeeper for the Liga Revelação 2020/21 season for Braga's youth team.[3] For the 2021–22 season, he was promoted to Braga B. He made his first senior debut with Braga in a 5–0 Taça de Portugal win over Moitense, coming on as a late substitute in the 85th minute.[4] He made his Primeira Liga debut with Braga in a 3–2 loss to Famalicão on 15 May 2022.[5]

International career

[edit]

Horníček is a youth international for the Czech Republic, having represented the Czech Republic U17s at the 2019 UEFA European Under-17 Championship.[6]

On the 31st May 2026, he made his senior debut in a 2-1 friendly win over Kosovo.[7]. After the game, he was confirmed in the 26-man squad heading for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[8]
