Christopher Téa Opéri (born 29 April 1997) is an Ivorian professional footballer who plays as a left-back for Süper Lig club İstanbul Başakşehir and the Ivory Coast national team.

Club career

[edit]

After years in the youth academy of Caen, Opéri was transferred to Châteauroux on 24 July 2017.[2] He made his first professional appearance with Châteauroux in a 2–1 Ligue 2 loss to Gazélec Ajaccio on 22 September 2017.[3]

On 2 June 2021, he signed a two-year contract with Gent in Belgium.[4] On 21 June 2022, Opéri's contract with Gent was terminated by mutual consent.[5]

On 29 June 2022, Opéri returned to France and signed a two-year contract with Le Havre.[6]

On 7 January 2025, Süper Lig club İstanbul Başakşehir signed Opéri for 3.5 years.[7]

International career

[edit]

Opéri made his debut for the Ivory Coast national team on 7 June 2024 in a World Cup qualifier against Gabon at the Amadou Gon Coulibaly Stadium. He substituted Ghislain Konan in the 86th minute of a 1–0 Ivory Coast victory.[8]

Opéri was included in the list of Ivorian players selected by coach Emerse Faé to participate in the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.[9][10]

On May 15, 2026, Opéri was integrated by Ivory Coast coach Emerse Faé in his list of 26 players in order to participate in the 2026 World Cup.[11]

Personal life

[edit]

Opéri was born in the Ivory Coast to an Ivorian father and French mother, and moved to France at a young age. His uncle Cyril Domoraud is a former professional footballer.[12]
