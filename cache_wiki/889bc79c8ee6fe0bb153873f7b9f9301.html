Guillermo Martínez Ayala (born 15 March 1995), commonly known as "Memote",[1] is a Mexican professional footballer who plays as a forward for Liga MX club Pumas and the Mexico national team.[2]

International career

[edit]

Martínez made his debut for the senior national team on 16 December 2023, in a friendly match against Colombia and scored a goal in a 2–3 defeat.[3]
