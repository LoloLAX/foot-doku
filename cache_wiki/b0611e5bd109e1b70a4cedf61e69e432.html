Haissem Yousry Fouad A Hassan (Egyptian Arabic: هيثم يسري فؤاد حسن; born 8 February 2002) is a professional footballer who plays as a forward for Segunda División club Real Oviedo. Born in France, he plays for the Egypt national team.

Club career

[edit]

Hassan signed his first professional contract with Châteauroux on 1 June 2018 at the age of 16.[3] He made his professional debut with Châteauroux in a 0–0 Ligue 2 tie with Paris FC on 19 October 2018.[4]

On 2 October 2020, Hassan signed a four-year contract with Villarreal CF, being initially assigned to the reserves in Segunda División B.[5] The following 9 August, he moved to Segunda División side CD Mirandés on a one-year loan deal.[6]

On 1 August 2023, Hassan renewed his contract with the Yellow Submarine until 2025, and was loaned to Sporting de Gijón in the second division for the season.[7] On 11 August 2024, he moved to Real Oviedo, also in the second division, on a three-year contract.[8]

International career

[edit]

Hassan was born in Paris, France to an Egyptian father and Tunisian mother.[9] He was a youth international for France.[10] On March 15, 2026, Hassan agreed to join the Egypt national football team ahead of the March International Window and to get a chance to play at the FIFA World Cup 2026.
