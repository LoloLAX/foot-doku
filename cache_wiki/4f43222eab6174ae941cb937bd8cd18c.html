Abduvohid Nematov (Uzbek: Abduvohid Neʼmatov, Абдувоҳид Неъматов; born 20 March 2001) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Nasaf and the Uzbekistan national team.

Career

[edit]

International

[edit]

Nematov made his debut for the Uzbekistan main team on 3 September 2020 in a Friendly match against Tajikistan.[2]
