Mohanad Mostafa Ahmed Abdelmonem Lasheen (Arabic: مهند مصطفى أحمد عبد المنعم لاشين; born 29 May 1996) is an Egyptian professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder for Egyptian Premier League club Pyramids and the Egypt national team.[3]

Career

[edit]

In March 2016, Mostafa was promoted from youth team to first team in Ismaily,[4] but he moved six month later to Alassiouty (now is called pyramids) Sport in the Egyptian Second Division and signed a 2-year contract.[5][6][7] Mostafa was part of the Alassiouty's (now is called pyramids) squad that promoted to 2017–18 Egyptian Premier League, he also scored the winning goal in the match that guaranteed the promotion for the club before 5 weeks from the end of the season.[8][9][10] In April 2017, Ismaily SC made negotiations with Alassiouty (now is called pyramids) to sign the player.[11][12]

International career

[edit]

On 2 December 2025, Lasheen was called up to the Egypt squad for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.[13]
