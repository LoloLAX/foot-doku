Ferran Torres García (Spanish pronunciation: [ˈferan ˈtores]; born 29 February 2000) is a Spanish professional footballer who plays as a forward or winger for La Liga club Barcelona and the Spain national team.

Torres began his career at Valencia, where he made his senior debut in 2017. He moved to Manchester City in 2020 and won the Premier League and the EFL Cup in the 2020–21 season. He departed for Barcelona in January 2022. Despite initially struggling in his first seasons at the club, in 2024–25, he helped Barcelona win a domestic treble of the La Liga title, the Copa del Rey, and the Supercopa de España. The following season marked Torres best-scoring season with his 21 goals being crucial to Barcelona winning another La Liga title.

He has represented Spain internationally at various youth levels, and made his debut for the senior team in 2020. He was named in Spain's squad for UEFA Euro 2020. He would then go on to represent the nation at the 2022 FIFA World Cup and Euro 2024, winning the latter tournament.

Club career

[edit]

Valencia

[edit]

Born in Foios, Valencian Community,[4] Torres joined Valencia CF's youth setup in 2006, aged six.[5][6] On 15 October 2016, while still a junior, he made his senior debut with the reserve team by coming on as a substitute for Grego in a 2–0 Segunda División B home loss against Mallorca B.[7]

Torres was promoted to the B-side ahead of the 2017–18 campaign, and scored his first senior goal on 26 August 2017 by netting his team's second in a 4–1 home win over Peralada-Girona B.[8] On 5 October, after being strongly linked to Barcelona and Real Madrid,[9] he renewed his contract, which increased his release clause to €25 million. He was also promoted to the first team on 1 January 2018.[10]

Torres made his first team debut on 30 November 2017, replacing fellow youth graduate Nacho Gil in a 4–1 home routing of Real Zaragoza, for the season's Copa del Rey.[11] He made his La Liga debut on 16 December, playing the last nine minutes in a 1–2 loss at Eibar, becoming the first player born in the 2000s to play in the league.[12][13] Torres made his European and Champions League debut on 23 October 2018, starting in a 1–1 draw against Young Boys.[14] He scored his first La Liga goal on 19 January 2019, ten minutes after coming on as a substitute in a 2–1 victory against Celta Vigo.[15] He remained on the bench as Valencia defeated Barcelona 2–1 in the 2019 Copa del Rey Final at the Estadio Benito Villamarín in Seville, on 25 May.[16]

On 5 November 2019, Torres scored his first Champions League goal, netting his team's last goal in a 4–1 home win against Lille,[17] becoming Valencia's youngest goalscorer in the competition.[18] On 23 November, Torres marked his 50th appearance in La Liga for Valencia with a 2–1 away defeat against Real Betis, becoming the youngest Los Ches player to play 50 league games at the age of 19 years and 254 days, breaking a 38-year-old record of Miguel Tendillo (19 years old and 351 days).[19]

Manchester City

[edit]

On 4 August 2020, English club Manchester City confirmed the signing of Torres on a five-year contract, until 2025,[20][21] for a reported transfer fee of €23 million (£20.8 million).[22] The club later revealed that Torres had inherited the shirt number 21 previously worn by club legend David Silva, a fellow Spanish player who also arrived from Valencia.[23][24] Torres made his debut in City's first game of the season, coming on as a substitute in a 3–1 away win against Wolverhampton Wanderers in the Premier League.[25] On 30 September, Torres scored his first goal for the club, in a 3–0 away win over Burnley in the EFL Cup.[26]

On 21 October 2020, he made his Champions League debut with Manchester City, scoring a goal in a 3–1 win against Porto.[27] Just a week later, Torres started and scored again in the Champions League in a 0–3 victory over Marseille, becoming the youngest Spanish player ever to score in three consecutive appearances in the competition, at 20 years and 241 days old.[28] On 28 November, Torres scored his first league goal for City in a 5–0 home win over Burnley.[29] On 14 May, Torres scored his first hat-trick for City in a 4–3 away league win over Newcastle United.[30]

Having fallen out of favour during the 2021–22 season, mainly due to injury, Torres agreed a transfer to Barcelona for €55 million (plus conditional add-ons worth €10 million) in December 2021, to be completed the following month dependent on Barça selling players from their squad. He had scored 16 goals in 43 appearances.[31]

Barcelona

[edit]

On 28 December 2021, both Barcelona and Manchester City confirmed that Torres had completed a permanent move to Barcelona, signing a five-year contract until June 2027, with a buyout clause of €1bn.[32][33] On 20 January 2022, Torres scored his first goal for Barcelona in a 3–2 loss against Athletic Bilbao at the end of extra time in the Copa del Rey round of 16.[34] On 20 March 2022, he scored a goal and assisted Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang in his first El Clásico, helping Barcelona to a 4–0 away victory against the league leaders.[35] During his first six months at the club, Torres made 25 appearances in all competitions, scoring seven goals.[36] Torres struggled with his form at the start of the 2022–23 season, and was often benched in favour of Ousmane Dembélé and new signings Raphinha and Robert Lewandowski.[37] On 19 February 2023, he put on a man-of-the-match performance in a 2–0 league win over Cádiz.[38][39] The following day, Torres revealed in an interview that he had "fallen into a bottomless well", and had worked with a psychologist during his form slump.[40][41]

On 21 January 2024, Torres scored his first hat-trick for Barcelona and provided an assist for João Félix on his 100th appearance for the club in a 4–2 away win against Real Betis.[42] Later that year, on 11 December, in a thrilling Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund, Torres delivered a stunning performance by scoring a decisive brace, securing a 3–2 away victory for Barcelona.[43] On 6 February 2025, he scored a hat-trick in the first half of a 5–0 away win over his former club Valencia in the Copa del Rey quarter-finals.[44] Two months later, on 2 April, he scored the only goal in a 1–0 away win over Atlético Madrid, clinching a 5–4 aggregate victory and a spot in the Copa del Rey final.[45] On 26 April 2025, he once again showed up to score a late equaliser against arch-rivals Real Madrid in the Copa del Rey final, forcing the game into extra time, where Barcelona secured the win thanks to Jules Koundé's winner. He was named MVP of the final and was the competition's top goal scorer.[46]

On 11 May 2025, Torres secured a vital hat-trick of assists for Barcelona in a 4–3 win against Real Madrid.[47] Four days later, the club said he had undergone surgery for appendicitis.[48] Later that year, on 6 December, he netted a first-half hat-trick in a 5–3 victory away to Real Betis, bringing his tally to 11 goals in 15 matches in the 2025–26 season which marked his best scoring run ever in La Liga.[49]

International career

[edit]

Youth

[edit]

Torres was a member of the Spain squad that won the 2017 UEFA European Under-17 Championship final over England, and was also a member of the squad that reached the final of the 2017 FIFA U-17 World Cup later that same year, losing out to the same opponent.[50][51] He was named in Spain's squad for the 2019 UEFA European Under-19 Championship in Armenia, scoring the winning penalty in the semi-final victory over France and both goals of the 2–0 final victory against reigning champions Portugal at the Vazgen Sargsyan Republican Stadium in Yerevan.[52] He made his debut for the Spain U21 side on 6 September 2019, in a 1–0 away win over Kazakhstan in a European Championship qualifier.[53][54]

Senior

[edit]

Early senior career

[edit]

Just 16 days after signing for Manchester City, Torres was called up to the senior Spain national team for the first time.[55] He debuted against Germany in the UEFA Nations League on 3 September 2020, playing the full 90 minutes in a 1–1 away draw and setting up a pre-assist for Spain's last minute equaliser.[56] Three days later, Torres scored his first senior international goal in a 4–0 win over Ukraine.[57][58] On 17 November 2020, he scored his first international hat-trick in a 6–0 victory over Germany.[59][60]

UEFA Euro 2020

[edit]

On 24 May 2021, Torres was included in Spain's 24-man squad for UEFA Euro 2020.[61][62] He scored the third goal of the Euro 2020 final 16 in the 76th minute of the game against Croatia, resulting in a 5–3 victory on 28 June.[63]

On 6 October, Torres scored both goals in Spain's 2–1 away victory over reigning European Champions Italy in the semi-finals of the 2020–21 UEFA Nations League.[64] In the final four days later, Spain suffered a 2–1 defeat against France.[65] With two goals, he was the joint–top scorer of the Nations League Finals, along with France's Karim Benzema and Kylian Mbappé, with the latter winning the Top Scorer Trophy due to having also provided two assists.[66]

2022 FIFA World Cup and UEFA Euro 2024

[edit]

On 11 November 2022, Torres was selected in the Spanish squad for the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar and scored his first ever World Cup brace against Costa Rica in a 7-0 victory.[67][68] In May 2024, he was named in the 26-man squad for the UEFA Euro 2024.[69] With Spain's victory against England in the final, Torres joined 12 other players in having won both youth and senior Euro titles, alongside his teammates Mikel Merino, Álvaro Morata, Nacho and Rodri.[70]

Style of play

[edit]

Regarded as a talented and highly promising young attacking winger in the media, Torres has been described as a traditional wide midfielder, due to his work-rate and penchant for running to the touchline, although he is also capable of cutting inside towards the centre of the pitch; due to his role, nationality, and playing style, Simone Lorini has likened him to Joaquín. Torres is known in particular for his pace, creativity, technique, and his close control at speed, which enables him to take on opponents and overload the flanks, while his height, athleticism, and heading ability also make him a strong aerial presence. Although he primarily plays on the right, he is capable of playing anywhere across the attacking line. In 2018, Spanish football journalist Guillem Balagué noted that Torres "is a dribbler, fast on the wing, can play on either wing, and he has got ability and intelligence. He can also play inside if needed, so he is a modern winger." The Valencia academy director of recruitment, José Giménez, instead stated: "He's powerful, quick, steady with the ball at his feet and strong in the air. He's unpredictable. When he's running with the ball at his feet he can go on inside or outside his man because he is two-footed. He can cross, finish and shoot the football". He has been often used as a super substitute since joining Barcelona.[71][72][73][74][75]

Personal life

[edit]

Between 2021 and 2023, Torres was in a relationship with Sira Martínez, daughter of football manager and former player Luis Enrique.[76]
