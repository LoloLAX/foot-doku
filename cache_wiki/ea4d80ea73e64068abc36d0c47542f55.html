Mike Louis Penders (born 31 July 2005) is a Belgian professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Premier League club Chelsea and the Belgium national team.

Club career

[edit]

Genk

[edit]

Penders is a youth product of Boorsem Sport and Bregel Sport, before moving to the academy of Genk in 2018.[3]

On 30 April 2021, he signed his first contract with Genk.[4] On 25 May 2022, he extended his contract until 2025.[5]

In the 2022–23 season, he was promoted to their reserve team Jong Genk.[2] He made his debut in a 4–1 victory against Lierse.[6][7]

On 23 August 2023, he again extended his contract until 2028.[8] He was made captain of Jong Genk in the 2023–24 season[7]

Following the departure of Maarten Vandevoordt and injury to Hendrik Van Crombrugge, Penders made his senior debut with the Genk first team in a 0–0 Belgian Pro League tie with Standard Liège on 28 July 2024.[9] At 18 years and 363 days, he was the third youngest goalkeeper to debut in the history of the league.[10]

In January 2025, Genk coach Thorsten Fink announced that Penders would be the team's starting goalkeeper for the remainder of the 2024–25 season.[11] He ended the season having played 21 league matches, including all ten matches in the Championship play-offs.[12]

Chelsea

[edit]

On 27 August 2024, it was announced that Penders would join Premier League club Chelsea in the summer of 2025.[7][13]

In June 2025, Penders was named in Chelsea's squad for the 2025 FIFA Club World Cup in the United States.[14] He was an unused substitute in the final on 13 July 2025.[15]

Loan to Strasbourg

[edit]

On 28 July 2025, Penders joined Ligue 1 club Strasbourg on a season-long loan deal.[16]

He made his debut for Strasbourg in their opening Ligue 1 fixture on 17 August, keeping a clean sheet against Metz.[17] He was an ever present in Strasbourg's runs to the semi-finals of both the Coupe de France[18] and UEFA Conference League.[19]

International career

[edit]

Penders is a youth international for Belgium, having represented the nation at under-17, under-18, under-19 and under-21 level.

In 2023, he was called up to the Belgium U19s where he acted as captain during the 2024 UEFA European Under-19 Championship qualification campaign.[20]

In 2025, Penders played four matches during qualification for the 2027 UEFA European Under-21 Championship.[21]

In August 2025, he received his first call up to the senior Belgium national football team.[22] On 18 November, he was an unused substitute in a 7–0 win over Liechtenstein in a 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifier.[23]

On 15 May 2026, he was named in Belgium's squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[24]
