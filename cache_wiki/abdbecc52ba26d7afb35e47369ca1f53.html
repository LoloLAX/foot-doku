Song Bum-keun (Korean: 송범근; born 15 October 1997) is a South Korean professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for K League 1 club Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors and the South Korea national team.

Club career

[edit]

Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors

[edit]

On 20 February 2018, Song made his professional debut in 2018 AFC Champions League against Kitchee SC.[4]

Shonan Bellmare

[edit]

On 18 December 2022, Song officially joined Japanese side Shonan Bellmare on a permanent transfer.[5]

Return to Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors

[edit]

On 2 January 2025, Song returned to Jeonbuk, joining the club as a free agent.[6]

International career

[edit]

Song has represented South Korea at all groups from under-18 to senior level. He was the team's first choice goalkeeper for the 2017 FIFA U-20 World Cup and the 2020 AFC U-23 Championship, winning the latter and being named Best Goalkeeper.[7] In 2021, he represented South Korea at the Tokyo Olympic Games.[8]

On 24 July 2022, Song made his debut for the senior Korea Republic national football team, keeping a clean sheet in a 3–0 win over Hong Kong in the 2022 EAFF E-1 Football Championship.[9] Later that year, he was a member of the Korean squad at the 2022 FIFA World Cup, serving as back-up to Kim Seung-gyu and Jo Hyeon-woo.[10]

On 16 May 2026, Song was named in South Korea's squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[11]

Private life

[edit]

In April 2024, it was announced by Antenna Entertainment that Song is in a romantic relationship with one of their artists, singer Lee Mijoo.[13][14] In February 2025, It was reported that the two had broken up.[15]
