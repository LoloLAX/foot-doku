Assane Diao (born 7 September 2005) is a Senegalese professional footballer who plays as a winger for Serie A club Como and the Senegal national team.

Club career

[edit]

Betis

[edit]

Diao is a youth product of Badajoz, Flecha Negra and Balón de Cádiz, before moving to the youth academy of Real Betis in 2021, on a contract until 2024.[4] Originally a midfielder, his attacking skills led to him being molded into a left winger, and he started playing with the reserves in the Segunda Federación in 2022.[5]

On 26 July 2023, Diao extended his contract with the club until 2027.[6] He made his senior and professional debut with the Verdiblancos as a late substitute in a 1–0 Europa League loss to Rangers on 21 September 2023.[7] Later that month, on 28 September, he scored his first goal on his La Liga debut in a 1–1 away draw against Granada.[8] A week later, on 5 October, he netted his first goal in European competitions in a 2–1 victory over Sparta Prague.[9] On 7 December, after coming on as a substitute, he scored his second La Liga goal, a 90+4' equalizer in a 2-2 draw against FC Barcelona.

Como

[edit]

On 7 January 2025, Betis announced Diao's transfer to Serie A side Como 1907.[10][11] The fee was reported at around €12 million.[12] On 23 February 2025, Diao scored a 77th minute goal vs Napoli to secure a 2–1 win vs the league leaders. As a result, Inter Milan overtook Napoli in the table.[13]

International career

[edit]

Born in Senegal, Diao moved to Spain at the age of three and has dual citizenship. He played for the nation's under-19 team at the 2023 UEFA European Under-19 Championship.[5]

On 19 March 2025, Diao's request to switch international allegiance to Senegal was approved by FIFA.[14]

Style of play

[edit]

Originally a midfielder, Diao is a strong, and powerful winger who excels at crossing the ball. Primarily right-footed, he is strong with both feet. He has a powerful technique, and strong heading ability.[15]

Personal life

[edit]

Diao has a twin brother, Ousseynou, who is also a youth footballer in Spain.[15]
