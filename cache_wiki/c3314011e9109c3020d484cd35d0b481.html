Ayoub Mohamed Al-Oui (Arabic: أيوب علوي‎; born 11 March 2005) is a Qatari professional footballer who plays as a right-back or center-back for Qatar Stars League club Al-Gharafa and Qatar national team.

Club career

[edit]

A youth product of Al-Gharafa, Al-Oui emerged as the team starter in the 2025–26 season and played an important role helping the team win the 2026 Amir of Qatar Cup.[1] Therefore, he was named the Best U-23 Player of the Season in the Qatar Stars League.[2][3]

International career

[edit]

On 8 October 2025, Al-Oui made his international debut with Qatar in the 2026 World Cup qualifiers game against Oman.[4] Later in the year, he featured at the 2025 FIFA Arab Cup.[5]

Personal life

[edit]

Born in Qatar, Al-Oui is of Tunisian descent.[6]
