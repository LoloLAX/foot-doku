Maurício Magalhães Prado (born 22 June 2001), simply known as Maurício, is a professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder or right midfielder for Série A club Palmeiras. Born in Brazil, he plays for the Paraguay national team.

Career

[edit]

Cruzeiro

[edit]

Maurício joined Cruzeiro on loan from Desportivo Brasil ahead of the 2019 season. In November 2019, Cruzeiro confirmed that they had signed Maurício for a fee around 800 thousand R$.[2]

Internacional

[edit]

On 5 November 2020, Maurício was presented at Internacional in an exchange of players between Inter and Cruzeiro. He scored his first goal against Fluminense FC.

On October 22, 2023, Maurício completed 150 games with the Inter shirt, in the historic victory over Santos, 7-1, in the Brazilian Championship.[3]

Palmeiras

[edit]

Maurício debuted on July 17, 2024, he played about 28 minutes in Rio de Janeiro. He dribbled, disarmed and showed his quality. In defeat, Palmeiras lost 1-0 to Botafogo, at the Nilton Santos Stadium, in a game valid for the 17th round of the Brasileirão.[4]

International career

[edit]

Maurício was born in Brazil and is of Paraguayan descent through his paternal grandmother.[5] On August 2023, he was called up to the Brazil U23s for a set of friendlies.[6]

On 9 February 2026, Maurício's request to switch international allegiance to Paraguay was approved by FIFA.[7][8]
