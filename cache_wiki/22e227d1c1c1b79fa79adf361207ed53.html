Alexander Olof Bernhardsson (born 8 September 1998) is a Swedish professional footballer who plays as a forward for 2. Bundesliga club Holstein Kiel and the Sweden national team.[2][3]

Club career

[edit]

On 22 January 2024, Bernhardsson signed a contract with 2. Bundesliga club Holstein Kiel until 30 June 2027.[4]

International career

[edit]

Bernhardsson made his full international debut for Sweden on 9 January 2023, playing for 90 minutes in a friendly 2–0 win against Finland.[5]

On 12 May 2026, Bernhardsson was named in the Sweden squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[6]
