Henrik Sælebakke Falchener (born 8 May 2003) is a Norwegian footballer who plays as a centre-back for Viking and the Norway national team.[2][3]

Club career

[edit]

Falchener was signed by Viking ahead of the 2025 season, after impressing for Egersund in the 2024 Norwegian First Division.[3] After an impressive 2025 season for both Falchener and Viking, where Falchener scored six goals, most of any centre-back in the league, Viking finished top of the table for the first time in over thirty years.[4]

International career

[edit]

On 31 March 2026, Falchener made his Norway national team debut in a friendly match against Switzerland.[5] On 21 May 2026, he was named in Norway's 2026 FIFA World Cup squad.[6]

On 21 May 2026, Falchener was included in the 26-man squad selected by Norway national team manager Ståle Solbakken for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[7]
