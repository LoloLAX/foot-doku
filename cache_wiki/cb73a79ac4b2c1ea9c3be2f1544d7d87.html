Akmal Vladimirovich Mozgovoy (Uzbek: Akmal Mozgovoy; born 2 April 1999) is an Uzbek professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Pakhtakor and the Uzbekistan national team.[1][2]

Career

[edit]

International

[edit]

Mozgovoy made his debut for the Uzbekistan main team on 3 September 2020 in a Friendly match against Tajikistan.[3]
