Julián Andrés Quiñones Quiñones (born 24 March 1997) is a professional footballer who plays as a forward and winger for Saudi Pro League club Al-Qadsiah. Born in Colombia, he represents the Mexico national team.[3] He scored the opening goal of the 2026 FIFA World Cup against the South Africa national soccer team at the 9th minute of the opening match of the tournament, held on 11 June 2026, at the Estadio Azteca in Mexico City.[4]

Club career

[edit]

Early career

[edit]

Quiñones commenced his career at Futbol Paz, an amateur football club located in Cali, Colombia. In the 2014-2015 season, he netted 50 goals across 38 matches, prompting Tigres UANL to recruit him for their U-20 team.[5]

In the Clausura 2016 tournament, Quiñones was sent on loan to Venados, an Ascenso MX club. He made his professional debut on 19 January 2016, scoring twice against Cruz Azul in a Copa MX match.

In June 2017, Quiñones joined BUAP on a one-year loan. In the Apertura 2017, he emerged as the third-highest scorer, finishing with nine goals. His impressive performance carried over into the Clausura 2018, where he scored another eight goals.

Quiñones returned to Tigres UANL for the Apertura 2018, but despite his impressive performances with Lobos BUAP, head coach Ricardo Ferretti kept him on the fringes of the squad.

Atlas

[edit]

In June 2021, Quiñones joined Atlas on a one-year loan.[6] Six months later, the move became permanent after Atlas acquired the player.[7] Quiñones won two consecutive league titles with Atlas, putting an end to the club's 70-year drought.

América

[edit]

In July 2023, América reached an agreement with Atlas to sign Quiñones.[8] During his brief spell at the club, he secured two league titles, bringing his individual total to six.

Al-Qadsiah

[edit]

In June 2024, Quiñones joined Saudi Arabian club Al-Qadsiah for a reported fee of $16 million, making him the most expensive player ever sold by a Mexican club.[9][10][11] He finished the 2024–25 season with 20 goals.

On 21 May 2026, he scored a hat-trick in a 5–1 away victory over Al-Ittihad, finishing the 2025–26 season as the league's top scorer with 33 goals, overtaking Ivan Toney on the final day of the campaign.[12] On 25 May, Quiñones renewed his contract with the club until 2029.[13]

International career

[edit]

Colombia

[edit]

Quiñones represented Colombia in youth competitions, first at the 2017 South American U-20 Championship and then at the 2018 Central American and Caribbean Games. However, in May 2023, he opted not to accept a call-up to the Colombia national team.[14][15]

Mexico

[edit]

In September 2023, Quiñones joined the training camp of the Mexico national team, having previously declared his intention to represent the country.[16] He completed his naturalization process in October and received his first call-up the following month.[17][18] Quiñones made his debut in a CONCACAF Nations League match against Honduras on 17 November.[19] He scored his first goal on 21 March 2024 in a semi-final match of the CONCACAF Nations League against Panama, scoring the 2nd goal of the game in the 43rd minute. Mexico went on to win the match 3–0. In 2026, he scored the first goal in the 2026 FIFA World Cup in Mexico City, in Azteca Stadium, being the first player in Concacaf to score the first goal in a World Cup. [20]

On the 11th June 2026, he opened up the world cup by scoring a goal at the 9th minutes of the first half infront of the home funs.[21] The world cup was hosted by his home country (Mexico), Canada and United State of America.[22]
