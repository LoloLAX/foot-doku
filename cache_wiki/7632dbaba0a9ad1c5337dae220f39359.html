Pavel Šulc (born 29 December 2000) is a Czech professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder for Ligue 1 club Lyon and the Czech Republic national team.

Šulc made over 150 appearances for Viktoria Plzeň, and was named the Czech Footballer of the Year in 2025.

Club career

[edit]

Viktoria Plzeň

[edit]

Šulc was promoted to the first team of Viktoria Plzeň in January 2020 but only played his first match on 17 January 2021 against FK Příbram.[2]

Scoring once each season in 2020–21 and 2021–22,[3] Šulc started the 2023–24 season with a hat-trick against České Budějovice.[4][5] He contributed to Viktoria Plzeň's 2023–24 UEFA Europa Conference League campaign, where they reached the quarter-final of a European competition for the first time.[6]

Loan spells

[edit]

On 29 January 2019, Šulc was loaned to FC Vysočina Jihlava in Czech National Football League, playing he played in 16 league matches and scored 3 goals.

On 3 July 2019, he was loaned to SFC Opava in Czech First League. In six months long loan he played in 15 league matches and in 1 Czech Cup match without scoring a goal.

On 26 January 2020, he was loaned to SK Dynamo České Budějovice in Czech First League. In one year long loan he played in 15 league matches and scored 3 goals.

In June 2022, he joined Czech First League side FK Jablonec on a season-long loan, reuniting with manager David Horejš who he had worked with at České Budějovice in his previous loan spell.[7]

Lyon

[edit]

On 4 August 2025, Šulc signed a four-year contract with Lyon in France.[8][9]

International career

[edit]

Šulc had played international football at youth levels that ranged from Czech Republic U18 to U21. He played a total of 15 matches for the U18–U20 teams and scored four times. For the U21 team, he played in 20 matches and scored two times.[10]

On 22 March 2024, Šulc debuted for the Czech senior squad in a friendly match against Norway.[11]

On 10 September 2024, Šulc scored his first goals for the Czech Republic, scoring twice in a 3–2 victory against Ukraine in the Nations League[12]
