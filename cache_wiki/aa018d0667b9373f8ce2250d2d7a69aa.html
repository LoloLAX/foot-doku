Armando González Alba (born 20 April 2003), nicknamed "Hormiga" (lit. 'Ant')[2] is a Mexican professional footballer who plays as a forward for Liga MX club Guadalajara and the Mexico national team.

Club career

[edit]

Guadalajara

[edit]

Tapatío

[edit]

González made his professional debut with Tapatío on 11 November 2020, coming on as a 89th-minute substitute against Pumas Tabasco.[3]

First team

[edit]

González made his first team, and Liga MX debut on 13 January 2024 coming on as a 85th-minute substitute in a 1–1 draw against Santos Laguna.[4] He finished his debut season with just 3 appearances and no goals.

González scored his first goal for Guadalajara in a match against Tijuana on 12 July 2024, where Guadalajara would go on to lose 2–4.[5]

On 26 July 2025, within the final minutes of the match, González would score a brace against Atlético San Luis, helping Chivas guarantee a 4–3 home victory.[6] On 25 October, González would score his first career hat-trick against Clásico Tapatío rivals Atlas in a 4–1 win.[7] He would go on to finish the Apertura tournament as the joint-top scorer with 12 goals,[8] also earning him the distinction of "Player of the Tournament."[9]

International career

[edit]

González was called up to the senior Mexico team by manager Javier Aguirre in November 2025 for friendly matches against Uruguay and Paraguay set to be played on the 15 and 18 of November respectively. González made his debut appearance for Mexico on 18 November against Paraguay with his side losing the match 2–1.[10] On 25 February 2026, González scored his debut goal with the national team against Iceland, the second in an eventual 4–0 win.[11]

Personal life

[edit]

González is the son of former professional footballer Armando González Bejarano.[12]
