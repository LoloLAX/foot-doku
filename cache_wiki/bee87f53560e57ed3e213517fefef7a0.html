Jamie Jaleel Jeremy Leweling (born 26 February 2001) is a German professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder, right winger, or forward for Bundesliga club VfB Stuttgart and the Germany national team.[3]

Club career

[edit]

Greuther Fürth

[edit]

Leweling made his professional debut for Greuther Fürth in the 2. Bundesliga on 28 July 2019, coming on as a substitute in the 85th minute for Tobias Mohr in 2–0 home loss against Erzgebirge Aue.[4] Later that year, on 7 December, he recorded his first goal and assist for the club in a 3–1 victory against Bochum.[5]

Following the club's promotion to the Bundesliga, he scored five goals during the 2021–22 season, opening his account with a goal against VfB Stuttgart in a 5–1 loss in the season's opening match.[6]

Union Berlin

[edit]

On 17 May 2022, it was confirmed that Leweling would join Bundesliga club Union Berlin for the 2022–23 season.[7] On 21 January 2023, he scored his first goal for the club in a 3–1 league victory against Hoffenheim.[8]

VfB Stuttgart

[edit]

On 13 July 2023, Leweling moved to VfB Stuttgart on loan.[9] On 27 January 2024, he scored his first goal in a 5–2 victory against RB Leipzig.[10] On 19 May 2024, Leweling signed for Stuttgart permanently with a contract until June 2028.[11] In December 2024, Leweling extended his contract with VfB Stuttgart until June 2029.[12] On 21 January 2025, he scored his first Champions League brace in a 3–1 away victory against Slovan Bratislava.[13]

International career

[edit]

Born in Germany, Leweling is of German, French, and Ghanaian descent.[14] His mother is German,[15] his father is Ghanaian.[16] In addition to German, Leweling holds both Ghanaian and French citizenships.[14] He was called up to the Ghana national team in October 2020, but he never made an appearance.[14]

In October 2024, Leweling was selected by coach Julian Nagelsmann to represent Germany, replacing injured Jamal Musiala, for the UEFA Nations League matches against Bosnia and Herzegovina and the Netherlands.[17] On 14 October, he made his debut whilst scoring the only goal in 1–0 victory against the latter opponent.[18]

On 21 May 2026, he was selected in Germany’s 26-man squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[19]
