Alejandro "Álex" Baena Rodríguez (born 20 July 2001) is a Spanish professional footballer who plays as a left midfielder or attacking midfielder for La Liga club Atlético Madrid and the Spain national team.

Club career

[edit]

Villarreal

[edit]

Baena was born in Roquetas de Mar, Almería, Andalusia,[2] and joined Villarreal's youth setup in 2011, from Roquetas.[3][4] He made his senior debut with the C-team on 21 December 2018, coming on as a second-half substitute in a 2–0 Tercera División home victory against Rayo Ibense.[5]

Ahead of the 2019–20 season, Baena was assigned to the reserves in Segunda División B, debuting as a starter on 14 September 2019 in a 3–0 victory against Llagostera.[6] On 13 July of the following year, he made his first-team league debut, replacing fellow youth graduate Manu Trigueros in a 1–2 home loss against Real Sociedad.[7]

Baena scored his first professional goal on 5 November 2020, netting his club's third in a 4–0 UEFA Europa League home routing of Maccabi Tel Aviv.[8] Seven days later, he renewed his contract until 2025.[9] On 19 August 2021, Baena moved to Segunda División club Girona on a one-year loan.[10] On 26 August 2022, he and teammate Nicolas Jackson were fully promoted to the main squad.[11] In the 2023–24 season, he became the top assist provider in La Liga with 14 assists.[12]

Atlético Madrid

[edit]

On 2 July 2025, Baena joined Atlético Madrid, signing a five-year-contract.[13]

International career

[edit]

Baena represented Spain at under-16, under-17, under-18, under-19 and under-20 levels, appearing in the 2018 UEFA European Under-17 Championship.[14] He also called up to the under-21 team for the 2023 UEFA European Under-21 Championship where Spain finished as runners-up.[15][16]

In August 2023, Baena received his first call-up to the Spain senior national team by head coach Luis de la Fuente, for two UEFA Euro 2024 qualifying matches against Georgia and Cyprus.[17] He went on to score his first goal for Spain on his debut against the latter opponent in a 6–0 victory.[18] In May 2024, Baena was named in the 26-man squad for the UEFA Euro 2024.[19]

In July 2024, Baena was included in the Spain U23 squad for the 2024 Summer Olympics in Paris.[20] He scored a goal from a free kick in the gold medal match against France,[21] where Spain won by 5–3 after extra time.[22]
