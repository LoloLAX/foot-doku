Hicham Boudaoui (Arabic: هشام بوداوي; born 23 September 1999) is an Algerian professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Ligue 1 club Nice and the Algeria national team.

Club career

[edit]

On 6 January 2018, Boudaoui made his professional debut for Paradou AC, coming as a second-half substitute in a league match against USM Alger.[3]

In September 2019, he joined Ligue 1 side Nice.[4]

International career

[edit]

Boudaoui made his debut for the Algeria national team on 27 December 2018 in a friendly against Qatar, as a starter.[5] He was named in Algeria's national team squad for the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations in Egypt.[6]

In December 2023, he was named in Algeria's squad for the 2023 Africa Cup of Nations.[7] He has also appeared for Algeria U20 and Algeria U23 teams.
