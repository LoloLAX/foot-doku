Eric Anders Smith (born 8 January 1997) is a Swedish professional footballer who plays as a centre back for Bundesliga club FC St. Pauli and the Sweden national team.[2]

Club career

[edit]

In January 2016, Smith joined Swedish team IFK Norrköping,[3] and moved abroad in June 2018 signing with Gent.[4]

Smith joined 2. Bundesliga club FC St. Pauli on loan for the second half of the 2020–21 season. FC St. Pauli secured an option to sign him permanently.[5]

International career

[edit]

On 12 May 2026, Smith was named in the Sweden squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[6] Smith made his full international debut on 1 June 2026 in a friendly 1–3 loss against Norway, playing for 45 minutes as a centre-back before being replaced by Alexander Bernhardsson.[7]

Personal life

[edit]

Smith was born in Sweden, and his father Anders is a former footballer.[8]
