Anas Saeed Badawi (Arabic: أنس بدوي; born 13 September 1997) is a Jordanian professional footballer who plays as a right-back for Jordanian Pro League club Al-Faisaly and the Jordan national team.

Club career

[edit]

Al-Ahli

[edit]

Badawi began his career at Al-Ahli, rising through the club's ranks.[1]

Sahab (loan)

[edit]

Badawi was a part of the Sahab squad that gained promotion in the 2019 Jordanian First Division League.[1]

Shabab Al-Aqaba

[edit]

Badawi joined Shabab Al-Aqaba during the 2021 Jordanian Pro League season and remained at the club for three season, being a major contributor during his time at the club.[1]

Al-Jazeera

[edit]

Badawi joined Al-Jazeera during the 2024–25 Jordanian Pro League, establishing himself as a key player for the club with a goal and three assists.[1][2]

Al-Faisaly

[edit]

On 20 May 2025, Badawi joined Al-Faisaly on a two-year contract.[3][4] He joined the club due to its special status within Jordan and his admiration to the fanbase.[5] He was sent off on his debut against his former club Al-Jazeera.[6]

International career

[edit]

On 24 August 2025, Badawi was called-up to the Jordan national team to participate in friendly matches against Russia and Dominican Republic, becoming one of four players to enter the national team for the first time.[7][8]
