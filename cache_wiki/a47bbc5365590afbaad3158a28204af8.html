Mohamed Amine Sbaï (born 5 November 2000) is a Moroccan professional footballer who plays as a winger for Ligue 1 club Angers and the Morocco national team.

Career

[edit]

Sbaï is a youth product of Nîmes, EP Vergèze and Le Crès.[1] He began his senior career with Alès in 2019.[2] He finished as top scorer of the shortened 2020–21 Championnat National 3 season with 9 goals.[3] For the 2021–22 season, he transferred to Sète where he scored 6 goals in 29 appearances.[4] He transferred to the Ligue 2 club Grenobole on 25 May 2022.[5]

On 30 August 2024, Sbaï joined Saudi Pro League club Al-Fateh on a two-year contract.[6]

International career

[edit]

Sbaï was called up to the Morocco national team for a set of friendlies in May 2026.[7] He was not initially part of the national squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup but on 11 June 2026 he was called up to replace an injured Abde Ezzalzouli.[8]

Personal life

[edit]

Born in Morocco. He holds both French and Moroccan nationalities.[9] His brothers, Salaheddine and Hatim Sbaï are also professional footballers.[10]
