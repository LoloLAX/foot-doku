Mohamed Naceur Manai (Arabic: محمد مناعي‎; born 25 October 2002) is a Qatari professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder for Qatar Stars League club Al-Shamal, on loan from Al-Sadd, and the Qatar national team.

Club career

[edit]

A product of the Al-Sadd academy, Manai was loaned to Al-Shamal in 2024.[2] At the 2024–25 Qatar Stars League, he was named the Best U-23 Player of the Season.[3][4]

International career

[edit]

In 2025, Manai was named in Qatar national team's squad to participate at the 2025 FIFA Arab Cup.[5]

Personal life

[edit]

Born in Qatar, Manai is of Tunisian descent.[6]
