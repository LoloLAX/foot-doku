Wilguens Raphael Polynice Paugain (born 24 August 2001) is a Haitian professional football player who plays as right-back for Zulte Waregem. A former youth international for France, he plays for the Haiti national team.

Career

[edit]

Paugain is a youth product of AS Nomexy Vincey and Épinal, before moving to Nancy's academy in 2015 where he finished his development. He debuted with their reserves in the Championnat National 3 in 2019.[2] On 22 July 2022, he transferred to the Cypriot club Akritas Chlorakas.[3] In 2023, he moved to the Latvian club FK Auda.[4] On 31 July 2024, he transferred to the Belgian club Zulte Waregem until 2026.[5] On 1 February 2025, he joined Zulte Waregem on loan for the second half of the 2024–25 season.[6]

International career

[edit]

Born in Haiti, Paugain moved to France at a young age and is a dual-citizen. He played for the France U18s in friendlies in 2018.[7] In March 2025, he was called up to the Haiti national team for a set of friendlies.[8]
