Mohammed Waheeb Abu Al-Shamat (Arabic: محمد وهيب أبو الشامات; born 11 August 2002) is a Saudi Arabian footballer who plays as a right-back for Al-Qadsiah and the Saudi Arabia national team.[1][2][3][4]

Club career

[edit]

Abu Al-Shamat started his career at the age of 8 in youth teams of Al-Ahli. He moved to Al-Qadsiah in 2017. On 5 November 2020, he signed his first professional contract with Al-Qadsiah.[5] On 13 August 2024, Abu Al-Shamat renewed his contract with Al-Qadsiah.[6]
