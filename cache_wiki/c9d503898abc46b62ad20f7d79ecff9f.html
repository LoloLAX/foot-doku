Víctor Muñoz Villanueva (born 13 July 2003) is a Spanish professional footballer who plays as a winger or forward for La Liga club Osasuna and the Spain national team.

Club career

[edit]

Early career

[edit]

Born in Barcelona, Catalonia, Muñoz joined FC Barcelona's La Masia at the age of eleven, after starting it out at CE Sant Gabriel.[2] In 2017, at the age of fourteen, he left the club and joined the youth academy of CF Damm.[citation needed]

Real Madrid

[edit]

In June 2021, Muñoz joined the youth teams of Real Madrid,[3] where he played in the UEFA Youth League. After making his senior debut with farm team RSC Internacional FC in 2022, he was promoted to the club's reserve team in Primera Federación in the following year.[citation needed]

During the 2024–25 season, Muñoz was regarded to have established himself as a starter for Castilla.[4] On 11 May 2025, he made his La Liga debut, substituting Vinícius Júnior in El Clásico match against former side Barcelona which ended in a 4–3 away defeat.[5]

On 18 June 2025, Muñoz made his FIFA Club World Cup debut in Real Madrid's first match under new head coach Xabi Alonso, coming on in the 80th minute for Vinícius Júnior in a 1–1 draw against Al Hilal.[6]

Osasuna

[edit]

On 11 July 2025, Muñoz signed a five-year contract with fellow top-tier club CA Osasuna, for a transfer fee of €5 million for 50% of his economic rights, with a further €1 million due in variables.[7]

International career

[edit]

On 27 March 2026, Muñoz made his debut for Spain and scored its third goal in a 3–0 friendly victory against Serbia at Estadio de la Ceramica.[8]

Style of play

[edit]

Muñoz plays as a winger or forward. American news website The Sporting News wrote in 2024 that "his speed is what makes him stand out the most and made him recently known as the fastest player in the Real Madrid squad".[9]
