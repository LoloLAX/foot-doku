Aurèle Florian Amenda (born 31 July 2003) is a Swiss professional footballer who plays as a centre-back for Bundesliga club Eintracht Frankfurt and the Switzerland national team.[2]

Club career

[edit]

Amenda signed his first professional contract in December 2021 with BSC Young Boys.[3][4] He made his professional debut for the club on 13 February 2022, replacing Wilfried Kanga in the 85th minute with a 3–1 league win over Basel.[5] He made three more appearances in the 2021–22 season, on 3 April 2022 against Basel,[6] on 9 April 2022 against Lausanne-Sport,[7] and on 22 May 2022 against Grasshoppers[8] finishing the season with four games in total.

At the start of the 2022–23 season, Amenda made his European debut on 28 July 2022 with Young Boys in a 3–0 win against Liepāja in the UEFA Europa Conference League qualifier match.[9] He played two matches for Young Boys in the 2022–23 Swiss Super League.[10]

On 7 February 2024, Amenda was announced at Eintracht Frankfurt from 1 July 2024, signing a five year contract with the club.[11]

International career

[edit]

Amenda represented Switzerland at youth international level.[1]

Amenda made his Switzerland national team debut on 15 November 2024 in a Nations League game against Serbia at Letzigrund. He played the full game in a 1–1 draw.[12]

Style of play

[edit]

Amenda is a centre-back with physical attributes including height and speed. He participates in a defensive play through his aerial and ground challenges, including tackling. In addition to these attributes, he moves the ball forward by dribbling into the opposition's half or using long-range passing. He is noted for maintaining composure in various situations and demonstrates having leadership qualities.[13]

Personal life

[edit]

Aurèle Amenda was born and raised in Switzerland. He is the child of a family originally from Cameroon.[14]
