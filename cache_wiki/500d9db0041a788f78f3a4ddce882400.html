Yazeed Moein Hasan Abu Laila (Arabic: يَزِيد مُعِين حَسَن أَبُو لَيْلَى; born 8 January 1993) is a Jordanian footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Jordanian Pro League side Al-Hussein and the Jordan national team.

Club career

[edit]

On 27 January 2023, Abu Laila joined Saudi First Division League club Al-Jabalain on loan.[4] On 3 July, Abu Laila renewed his loan with Al-Jabalain on a season-long loan.[5]

On 14 July 2024, Abu Laila signed for Al-Hussein SC.

International career

[edit]

Abu Laila made his debut for the Jordan national team on 1 June 2017 in a 1–0 defeat to Iraq in friendly at Basra International Stadium.[6]

He was a part of the called up squad that reached the final of the 2023 edition of the Asian Cup, playing every match.
