Salah Zakaria Hassan (born 24 April 1999) is a Qatari professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Al-Duhail and the Qatar national team.

Club career

[edit]

Zakaria began his professional career with Al-Gharafa in 2019. In January 2021 he joined Al-Duhail.[1]

International career

[edit]

In August 2022, Zakaria made his first appearance for the Qatar national team against Jamaica. The match ended 1-1.[2]

  • Qatari Stars Cup: 2022-23
  • Emir of Qatar Cup: 2022
  • Qatar Cup: 2023

  • AFC Asian Cup: 2023
