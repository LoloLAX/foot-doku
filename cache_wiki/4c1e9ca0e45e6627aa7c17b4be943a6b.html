José Manuel Alberto López (born 6 December 2000), commonly known as Flaco López, is an Argentine professional footballer who plays as a striker for Campeonato Brasileiro Série A club Palmeiras and the Argentina national team.

Club career

[edit]

López started his career at the age of seven with Club El Progreso in Saladas.[3] Six months later, soon before a trial in front of their scouts, the player was signed by Boca Juniors.[3] At the age of nine, López joined Independiente; remaining until 2016, he notably trained with Ariel Holan's first-team squad at one point.[3] 2017 saw López head to Lanús, initially featuring at youth level before being called up mid-season ahead of the 2018–19 Primera División resumption.[3] However, in that period, López was loaned to Liga Tresarroyense side Colegiales de Tres Arroyos in 2019.[3][4][5] He finished his stint there as the club's top scorer.[3]

López was promoted into Luis Zubeldía's senior squad in late-2020, appearing on the substitute's bench for Copa de la Liga Profesional encounters with Aldosivi and Defensa y Justicia.[2][6] López, in the aforementioned competition, made his professional debut on 3 January 2021 during a draw on the road against Patronato, after he was substituted on in place of Gonzalo Torres with twenty-five minutes left.[2][7] On his second appearance, López scored his first goal during a win over Rosario Central on 9 January.[2]
