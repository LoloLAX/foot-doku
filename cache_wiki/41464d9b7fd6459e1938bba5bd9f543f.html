Álvaro David Montero Perales (born 29 March 1995) is a Colombian professional footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Argentine Primera División club Vélez Sarsfield, on loan from Categoría Primera A club Millonarios and the Colombia national team.[2]

International

[edit]

Montero made his Colombia national team debut on 3 June 2019, in a friendly against Panama, as a 77th-minute substitute for David Ospina.[3]
