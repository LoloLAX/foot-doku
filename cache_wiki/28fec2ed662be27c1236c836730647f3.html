Mohamed Elias Achouri (Arabic: مُحَمَّد إِلْيَاس عَاشُورِيّ; born 10 February 1999) is a professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for Danish Superliga club Copenhagen. Born in Réunion, an overseas department of France, he plays for the Tunisia national team.

Club career

[edit]

On 26 August 2021, Achouri joined Liga Portugal 2 club Trofense on loan from Estoril.[1] On 31 August 2022, Achouri joined Danish Superliga side Viborg FF on a deal until June 2025.[2]

Achouri made the move to 15-time Danish Superliga champions Copenhagen on 24 July 2023, signing a four-year deal with the Lions. This was at the time the biggesst sale of Viborg FF ever.[3]

International career

[edit]

Achouri was born in Saint-Denis, Réunion to a Tunisian father and an Algerian mother.[4] He debuted for the Tunisia national team in a 4–0 win over Equatorial Guinea on 2 June 2022.[5]
