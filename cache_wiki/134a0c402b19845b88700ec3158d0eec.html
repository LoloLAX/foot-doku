Azarias Emmanuel Londoño González (born 21 June 2001) is a Panamanian professional footballer who plays as forward for Universidad Católica, on loan from Alianza, and the Panama national team.

Club career

[edit]

Alianza

[edit]

Londoño began his senior career with Alianza in 2020.

2021: Loan to O&M

[edit]

In 2021, he joined the Dominican club O&M on loan for the season.

2022: Return to Alianza and first league title

[edit]

Returning to Alianza, He helped them win the 2022 Liga Panameña de Fútbol.[1]

2022–23: Loan to Comunicaciones

[edit]

On 18 August 2022, he was loaned out to the Liga Nacional de Fútbol de Guatemala side Comunicaciones.[2]

2023–24: Loan spells at Torreense and Comunicaciones

[edit]

On 12 July 2023, he was then loaned to the Liga Portugal 2 side Torreense.[3]

On 27 January 2024, he returned again to Comunicaciones on loan for the 2024 season.[4]

2025–26: Loan to Universidad Católica

[edit]

On 8 January 2025, he joined the Ecuadorian Serie A club Universidad Católica on loan.[5]

International career

[edit]

Youth

[edit]

Londoño was called up to the Panama U20 for the 2022 Maurice Revello Tournament.[6]

Senior

[edit]

Londoño debuted with the made the Panama national team in a friendly 2–0 win over Bahrain on 27 September 2022.[7] He was part of the squad for the 2023 CONCACAF Gold Cup.[8] He again made the final Panama squad for the 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup.[9]
