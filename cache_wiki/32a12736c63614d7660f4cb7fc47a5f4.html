Fredrik André Bjørkan (born 21 August 1998) is a Norwegian professional footballer who plays as a left-back for Eliteserien club Bodø/Glimt and the Norway national team.[1]

Club career

[edit]

Bodø/Glimt

[edit]

Bjørkan was born in Bodø, Nordland.[2] He started playing for hometown club Bodø/Glimt as a youth and made his first professional appearance at age 17, on 13 April 2016, in the Norwegian Football Cup, against IF Fløya, which Bodø/Glimt won 6–0.[citation needed] He made his league debut for Bodø/Glimt on 17 April 2016 against Molde where Bodø/Glimt lost 2–1.[citation needed]

Hertha BSC

[edit]

On 1 December 2021, Bjørkan signed for Hertha BSC on a contract until 2025,[3] joining at the beginning of the January transfer window.[1] Bjørkan moved back to Bodø/Glimt in January 2023.[4]

Feyenoord (loan)

[edit]

On 29 August 2022, Feyenoord announced that it had signed Bjørkan on loan with an option to buy.[5] The loan agreement was disbanded in January 2023.[4]

International career

[edit]

In May 2021, Bjørkan was called up to the Norwegian national team for the first time.[6] He made his debut during that international window on June 6, 2021, in a friendly match against Greece. He came on as a substitute for Birger Meling in a game Norway lost 2–1.[7][8]

On May 21, 2026, he was included by Norway head coach Ståle Solbakken in a 26-man squad selected to participate in the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[9]

Personal life

[edit]

Fredrik André is the son of former player Aasmund Bjørkan.[10]
