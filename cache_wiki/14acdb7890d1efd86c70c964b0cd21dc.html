Aleksandar Pavlović (Serbian Cyrillic: Александар Павловић; born 3 May 2004) is a German professional footballer who plays as a defensive midfielder for Bundesliga club Bayern Munich and the Germany national team. He is known for his passing accuracy, vision and composure on the ball.[4]

Club career

[edit]

Youth career

[edit]

Pavlović was an academy of Bayern Munich youth product. He joined Bayern Munich in 2011 when he was seven years old from SC Fürstenfeldbruck.[2] On 23 July 2021, Pavlović extended his contract until 2022.[5] On 3 May 2022, he signed a contract with Bayern Munich until 2024.[6] That same month, he started training with the senior squad of Bayern Munich for the first time, subsequently appearing on the bench for a pair of Bundesliga matches.[7]

Senior career

[edit]

Pavlović played for the senior Bayern Munich in the pre-season in the summer of 2023.[8] He made his Bundesliga debut as a substitute in an 8–0 victory against Darmstadt 98 on 28 October 2023.[9] On 1 November 2023, he signed a professional contract with Bayern Munich until 2027.[10] On 4 November, he provided his first assist in Bundesliga to the third goal scored by Harry Kane in a 4–0 away victory against Borussia Dortmund.[11] One week later, Pavlović made his first start in a 4–2 victory against 1. FC Heidenheim.[12] On 29 November, he made his Champions League debut, coming off the bench in the 64th minute in a goalless draw against Copenhagen.[13] On 27 January 2024, he scored his first goal in a Bundesliga 3–2 away victory against Augsburg.[14] Later that year, on 16 June, Pavlović extended his contract until 2029.[15]

In the 2024–25 season, he achieved his first Bundesliga title with the club.[16] On 15 April 2026, he scored his first Champions League goal in a 4–3 win over Real Madrid in the quarter-finals.[17]

International career

[edit]

Born in Germany to a Serbian father and a German mother,[18][19][20] Pavlović holds dual citizenship.[21] In September 2021, he was called up to the Serbia U18 for a set of friendly matches.[22] In November 2023, Pavlović was called up to the Germany national under-20 football team.[23]

On 14 March 2024, Pavlović was called up for the German senior squad by head coach Julian Nagelsmann for the friendly matches against France and the Netherlands.[24] However, he missed both fixtures due to suffering from tonsillitis.[25]

Pavlović was selected in the German preliminary squad for the UEFA Euro 2024.[26][27] He made his senior debut on 3 June in a friendly match against Ukraine prior to the tournament.[28] Pavlović was confirmed in Germany's final 26-man squad,[29] but had to withdraw due to an illness.[30] On 7 September 2024, he scored his first goal for Germany in a 5–0 victory against Hungary during the UEFA Nations League.[31]

On 21 May 2026, he was selected in Germany’s 26-man squad for the 2026 FIFA World Cup.[32]
